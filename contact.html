Is this a real detailing centre?+

No. CARBON DETAIL is a fictional brand and this website is a design concept. The services, prices, reviews and address are examples.

Is my booking confirmed when I click?+

No. Clicking only opens a WhatsApp chat with a draft message. You decide whether to send it. No appointment is reserved by this website.

How are the concept prices calculated?+

The displayed prices are illustrative starting prices in AED. For a real service, the final scope would depend on vehicle size, materials and condition.
